Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your consultation

Am I suitable for laser eye surgery?
Most people with a stable prescription over the age of 18 are suitable for laser eye surgery. During your free virtual consultation, our experts will assess your suitability based on your prescription, corneal thickness, and overall eye health. The vast majority of patients are suitable for at least one of our treatments.
Does laser eye surgery hurt?
Numbing drops are applied before treatment so the procedure itself is not painful. Most patients describe mild pressure or awareness but no pain. Any mild discomfort afterward typically settles within a few hours.
How long does recovery take?
Recovery varies by treatment type. LASIK and ReLEx SMILE patients often return to normal activities within 24–48 hours. LASEK recovery typically takes 1–2 weeks. Your surgeon will guide you through the full aftercare process.
What are the finance options available?
We offer 0% finance options from as little as £25 per month, with plans ranging from 10 months to longer terms. Finance is subject to status — your consultation team can walk you through the options best suited to your circumstances.
Is laser eye surgery permanent?
Yes, the corneal reshaping achieved in laser eye surgery is permanent. However, age-related changes (such as presbyopia, typically after 40) can still affect vision over time. Treatments like PRESBYOND are designed to address these age-related changes.
